# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in Conake OS discussions, contributions, reviews, and
project spaces is expected to treat others with respect.

Be patient with different experience levels and communication styles. Give
specific, constructive feedback about the work. Respect privacy, accept
reasonable boundaries, and acknowledge and correct mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, and publishing someone's private information without consent are
not acceptable. Do not use project spaces to intimidate or target others.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, ask participants to change
their behavior, or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the
severity and context of the conduct, protect affected people, and allow a
fair opportunity to clarify relevant facts. A maintainer involved in a
complaint should not decide its outcome alone when another maintainer is
available.

Report conduct concerns using a private contact method a maintainer has
chosen to publish. If none is available, request a private contact route
without naming affected people or posting incident details in a public
issue. Maintainers should keep reports confidential as far as reasonably
possible and explain any necessary limits before sharing them.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents Conake OS
in public. It does not prohibit respectful disagreement about technical
decisions.
